# Instructions: AI Capability Scenario Stress-Test

## Overview
The AI Capability Scenario Stress-Test helps individuals and teams explore how their existing AI capability profile might perform under plausible future or disruptive scenarios.

## Capability Development
Capability is developed through use by:
- Engaging in reflective assessment of current strengths and gaps.
- Discussing scenario-based pressures and their impact on institutional stability.
- Identifying small, stabilizing actions to improve resilience.

## Governance Support
Governance is supported through lightweight, optional fields that allow you to document:
- **Capability Notes**: How AI capability is being developed through the specific workflow or stress-test.
- **Governance Notes**: Assumptions, risks, and human review notes related to the scenario.

## Optional Fields
All governance-related fields are optional. They are designed to make your professional judgement visible and reviewable without creating administrative friction.
