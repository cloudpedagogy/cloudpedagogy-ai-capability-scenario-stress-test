# AI Capability Scenario Stress-Test — User Instructions

---
### 2. What This Tool Does
This tool applies intense simulated pressure to an institution's capability baseline. By launching hypothetical high-risk scenarios (e.g., a massive data breach or a rapid vendor failure), it exposes which capability domains will buckle first under real-world strain.

---
### 3. Role in the Ecosystem
- **Phase:** Phase 3 — Capability System
- **Role:** Testing organizational resilience against AI-driven risk scenarios.
- **Reference:** [../SYSTEM_OVERVIEW.md](../SYSTEM_OVERVIEW.md)

---
### 4. When to Use This Tool
- During tabletop risk exercises or executive governance workshops.
- To validate if an organization is truly ready to deploy a high-stakes AI tool.
- To stress-test the assumptions outlined in a recent capability or maturity audit.

---
### 5. Inputs
- Requires a baseline capability dataset (usually exported from the **Gaps & Risk** diagnostic tool).
- Requires selection of a predefined risk scenario (e.g., Academic Integrity Crisis).

---
### 6. How to Use (Step-by-Step)
1. In the Setup view, load your organizational baseline dataset.
2. Select a high-impact risk scenario from the provided catalogue.
3. Advance to the Results view to observe the impact.
4. Review the "Scenario Resilience Index" to see if the overall institution holds up (Robust vs Critical).
5. Analyze the "Domain Impact Matrix" to quickly spot which specific domains suffered a "High Impact" score drop under pressure.
6. Review the discussion prompts and export a PDF/JSON trace for the continuity team.

---
### 7. Key Outputs
- A "Resilience Index" score representing structural stability under crisis.
- Highly actionable, scenario-specific discussion prompts and "small stabilisers".

---
### 8. How It Connects to Other Tools
- **Upstream:** Relies on the baseline scores generated by the **Capability Assessment** or **Gaps & Risk** tools.
- **Downstream:** Identifies fragile systems that may require redesigning in the **Workflow Governance Designer**.

---
### 9. Limitations
- Does not predict actual future events; it only models theoretical stress based on predefined heuristics.
- If the imported baseline is overly optimistic, the stress test will return a false sense of security.

---
### 10. Tips
- Focus less on the final score, and more on the domains marked "High Impact." That is where you should dedicate your training budget.
